Do I need ABS compliance for my Ashwagandha-based cosmetic startup?

U
Jurisdiction: INDIA Category: Cosmetic (Ayush)
High Confidence

Executive Summary

Yes, as a commercial entity utilizing Indian biological resources (Ashwagandha) for commercial utilization (cosmetics), you are subject to Access and Benefit Sharing (ABS) compliance under the Biological Diversity Act, 2002.

Legal Position

Section 7 of the BD Act mandates that prior intimation to the State Biodiversity Board (SBB) is required for Indian entities accessing biological resources for commercial utilization.

Exemptions Not Applicable

Value-added products (VAP) exclude raw herbs. Since Ashwagandha extracts involve commercial utilization, the Section 40 VAP exemption generally does not apply to the raw material sourcing phase.

Required Next Steps

  1. Identify the exact source (cultivator/forest) of the Ashwagandha.
  2. File Form 1 or notify the respective State Biodiversity Board (SBB).
  3. Execute an ABS agreement before commercialization.

Uttarakhand High Court ruling on ABS applicability to Indian companies.
